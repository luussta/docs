# Plans and credits

AI actions use credits. Text analysis, image generation, and storyboard rendering can have different costs. The usage view shows the action, project, person, time, and credits consumed; use filters to narrow the activity.

Plans and checkout are handled through Whop. From account settings you can compare plans, change plans, open the billing portal, or cancel a plan. A cancellation affects future renewals according to the billing terms shown in Whop.

For local testing, billing and authentication can be disabled and usage can run against the local configuration. Do not use local credentials or test data in a production workspace.
