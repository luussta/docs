# Storyboard

Storyboard mode converts scenes into landscape frames. Before rendering, choose visual direction such as lighting, focus, camera angle, lens, shot size, movement, composition, color, and aspect ratio.

## References and continuity

Frames identify characters and locations from the scene and use their approved references. When available, the next frame also uses the previous frame to help maintain wardrobe, blocking, lighting, and composition continuity. A drawing-mode reference can guide the visual style.

## Render workflow

1. Review the frame prompt and visual direction.
2. Confirm the referenced characters, locations, and optional drawing image.
3. Render the frame.
4. Click a frame to inspect it full screen, then regenerate only when needed.

Rendering consumes credits. Keep prompts specific and reuse approved references instead of uploading duplicates.
